# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

Ocello moves files directly between your own devices on your local network. It is designed to collect nothing.

## What Ocello does not do
- No accounts, sign-in or registration.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
- No connection to the internet or to any server run by us or anyone else. Fonts and images are built into the app.
- Your files are never uploaded anywhere. They go only from the sending device to the receiving device you choose.

## What stays on your device
- **Settings**: on/off switches, the number of parallel streams, the save folder you chose, and the list of devices you paired with (name and key fingerprint).
- **Device key**: a private key and certificate created on first launch, used to encrypt transfers. It never leaves the device.
- **Activity list**: kept in memory only and cleared when the app closes.

On Android, app data is excluded from Google cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

## What other devices on your network can see
While Ocello is open, devices on the same network can see this device's name (the Windows computer name, or "Android phone") and platform, so they can offer to send to it. They cannot send anything without your approval or your pairing code. Turn off **Automatic discovery** and **Allow incoming transfers** in Settings on networks you do not trust.

## Android permissions
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet, network state | Talk to the other device on your local network |
| Notifications | Show progress for background transfers |
| Foreground service, wake lock | Keep a transfer running while you use other apps |
| All files access | Only if you choose a save folder outside Downloads |
